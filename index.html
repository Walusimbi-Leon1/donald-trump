The Biography

This is the story of a man who defied every expectation. Donald John Trump rose from the middle-class neighborhoods of Queens, New York, to build a global real estate empire, dominate American television, and ultimately reshape the political landscape of the world's most powerful nation — not once, but twice.

This comprehensive biography traces his journey from the son of a Brooklyn real estate developer, through the glittering towers of Manhattan, the boardrooms of The Apprentice, the campaign trail, and two historic presidencies. It examines the leadership style, the business philosophy, and the unyielding determination that made him one of the most consequential figures of the twenty-first century.
