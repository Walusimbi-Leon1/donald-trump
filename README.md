## About the Book

*Donald Trump* is a comprehensive biography of the 45th and 47th President of the United States — a real estate mogul, television personality, and the most consequential American political figure of the 21st century. This book examines his rise from Queens to the White House, his leadership style, and his impact on the world stage.

### What's Inside

- **Early Life** — Queens, family, education at Wharton
- **The Businessman** — Trump Organization, building a real estate empire
- **The Brand** — Trump Tower, licensing, becoming a household name
- **The Entertainer** — The Apprentice, celebrity status, pop culture
- **The 45th President** — Key policies, executive orders, governing style
- **Foreign Policy** — Standing up on the world stage, negotiations
- **The Economy** — Tax cuts, job growth, trade deals
- **The Return** — 2024 campaign and the 47th presidency
- **Famous Quotes** — 60+ memorable quotes from speeches, tweets, and books
